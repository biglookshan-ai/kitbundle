Unified add-to-cart button: before / after

Normal products and KitBundle products now use the same add-to-cart button: yellow with dark text, all caps (Geist 12px, 0.12em letter spacing, after Moment), no cart icon, black sweep from the left on hover. Quantity on the left, button on the right. The button shows the total and updates as you change options; when bundle parts, add-ons or free gifts are included it reads "ADD N ITEMS TO CART".

Before = live store screenshots, 17 Nov 2026 (morning). After = the same products after the change. Screenshots are 1:1 (desktop column 1280 px wide, phone 375 px). Live on the store since 17 Nov 2026.

How the label works

  • Only the main product: ADD TO CART - £total (buying 2 of the same product still says ADD TO CART, amount doubles)
  • Anything else going in (bundle parts, add-ons, free gifts): ADD N ITEMS TO CART - £total. Items = every item in the product or bundle × quantity + add-ons + gifts
  • Pre-order: PRE-ORDER NOW - £total (or PRE-ORDER N ITEMS - £total)
  • Sold out / not available: label only, no amount, cannot be clicked
